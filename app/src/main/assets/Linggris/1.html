Author's Purpose & Tone

A common UTBK question asks: "What is the author's purpose in writing the text?" or "What is the tone of the passage?"

1. Author's Purpose (Tujuan Penulis)

  • To inform/explain: Giving facts and information (usually neutral).
  • To persuade/argue: Trying to make the reader agree with their opinion.
  • To criticize: Pointing out problems or bad things about a topic.

2. Tone (Nada Tulisan)

Pay attention to the adjectives the author uses.

  • Objective/Neutral: Just stating facts, no personal feelings.
  • Critical: Expressing disapproval.
  • Optimistic: Hopeful and positive about the future.
  • Pessimistic: Expecting the worst to happen.
