Reading Comprehension Strategies

In the English Literacy test, you don't need to read every single word. Time is your enemy!

1. Skimming for Main Idea:
Read the first and last sentence of each paragraph to grasp the general topic.

2. Scanning for Details:
Look at the question first! Find the keywords, then scan the text specifically for those keywords.

3. Vocabulary in Context:
If you don't know a word, guess its meaning from the words around it (context clues).
